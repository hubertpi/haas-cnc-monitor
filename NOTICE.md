Haas CNC Monitor is derived from hubertpi/fanuc-cnc-monitor 0.3.4,
commit 62087806e71fa55fccee8b2b5ee3141c567cdb04, under Apache-2.0.
The web interface, persistence, MQTT journal, scheduling and utilization
code were retained and adapted. Haas collectors are new implementations.

TrakHound/MTConnect.NET-Haas was inspected as a protocol reference.
Its source implementation is not bundled. No FANUC FOCAS binding or library
is included. Haas and FANUC are trademarks of their respective owners.
This is an independent community project, not a Haas product.
